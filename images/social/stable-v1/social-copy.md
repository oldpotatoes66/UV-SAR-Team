# UV-SAR-TEAM 正式版发布 / Stable Release

## 中文社交媒体文案

UV-SAR-TEAM 首批稳定版本正式发布：

- UV-K1：v1.0
- UV-K5 / UV-K6：v1.1

这是面向公益救援、户外协作和无线电测向训练的免费开源固件。两个机型现已具备统一的核心能力：正常语音收发、Yaesu ARTS 互通、SAR 测向、Bandscope、独立测向训练信标、常驻功率显示、低电量发射保护、60 秒 PTT 超时，以及失联声音和灯光提醒。

新加入的独立训练信标使用 `F+7` 进入，按 `MENU` 启停。它每 30 秒以最低训练功率发送呼号 CW 和 3 秒测向信号，支持忙信道避让、低电禁发和 30 分钟自动停止，方便一个人完成测向训练。

无需安装软件，可通过网页刷写固件、配置呼号和批量管理频道：

https://sar.trailspud.com/

项目免费开源，欢迎救援队、无线电爱好者测试、学习和共同改进。

安全提示：请遵守当地频率、呼号和发射规定；本项目不能作为唯一救援通信、定位或生命安全设备。

## English social copy

UV-SAR-TEAM stable firmware is now available:

- UV-K1: v1.0
- UV-K5 / UV-K6: v1.1

This free and open-source firmware is built for volunteer search and rescue, outdoor team coordination, and radio direction-finding practice. Both radio families now share the same practical core: normal voice communication, Yaesu ARTS interoperability, SAR direction finding, Bandscope, an independent training beacon, persistent power display, low-battery TX protection, a 60-second PTT timeout, and audible plus visual link-loss alerts.

Press `F+7` to open the independent training beacon and `MENU` to start or stop it. It sends the configured callsign in CW and a three-second direction-finding signal every 30 seconds at the lowest training power. Busy-channel avoidance, low-battery blocking, and a 30-minute automatic stop are built in.

Flash firmware, configure callsigns, and manage channels directly on the web:

https://sar.trailspud.com/

Free to use, learn, test, and contribute.

Safety: follow local frequency, callsign, power, and transmission rules. This project must never be the sole rescue communication, location, or life-safety system.
