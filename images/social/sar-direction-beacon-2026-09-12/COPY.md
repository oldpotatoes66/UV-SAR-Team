# SAR-TEAM 新功能宣传文案｜2026-09-12

## 图片 1：新功能发布

### 中文

SAR-TEAM 于 2026 年 9 月 12 日完成两项面向搜救与户外训练的新功能：SAR 无线电测向和独立测向训练信标。

### English

On September 12, 2026, SAR-TEAM added two practical tools for search-and-rescue and outdoor radio training: SAR direction finding and an independent direction-finding training beacon.

- `F + 5`: open SAR direction finding with live dBm, relative strength, peak hold, and signal trend.
- `F + 7`: open the independent training beacon for solo or team direction-finding practice.
- Supports UV-K1, UV-K5, and UV-K6. Free and open source.

Project: https://github.com/oldpotatoes66/UV-SAR-Team

## 图片 2：快速使用方法

### 中文

两个新功能，无需复杂学习即可开始使用。

SAR 测向：

1. 在主界面选择目标频率。
2. 按 `F + 5` 进入 SAR 测向。
3. 缓慢转动天线或改变站位，观察 dBm、信号条和趋势箭头。
4. dBm 越接近 0，信号越强；应从多个位置交叉判断方向，不能只依靠箭头。

测向训练信标：

1. 在合法频率上使用低功率，并确认训练环境可控。
2. 按 `F + 7` 进入训练信标。
3. 按 `MENU` 启动或停止，按 `EXIT` 立即退出。
4. 启动后每 30 秒发送呼号 CW 和 3 秒训练载波；30 分钟后自动停止。

信标具有忙信道避让、低电禁发和非法频率禁发保护。请遵守当地频率、呼号、功率及发射规定。

### English

Two new functions designed for direct field use.

SAR direction finding:

1. Select the target frequency on the main screen.
2. Press `F + 5` to enter SAR direction finding.
3. Slowly rotate the antenna or move to another position while watching dBm, the strength bar, and the trend arrow.
4. A dBm value closer to zero means a stronger signal. Take bearings from multiple positions; never rely on the arrow alone.

Direction-finding training beacon:

1. Select a legal frequency and low power in a controlled training area.
2. Press `F + 7` to enter the beacon.
3. Press `MENU` to start or stop; press `EXIT` to leave immediately.
4. The beacon sends the configured callsign in CW plus a three-second training carrier every 30 seconds and stops automatically after 30 minutes.

Busy-channel avoidance, low-battery TX blocking, and invalid-frequency protection are built in. Follow local frequency, callsign, power, and transmission regulations.
